SECTION 04 // OPERATIONAL BLUEPRINT

Starting an Online Store? Start Here.

A systematic launch blueprint helping you navigate platform decisions, minimize setup friction, and organize store operations.

01 Milestone 1

Choose Your Business Model

Determine inventory reality before choosing software. Clarify physical warehousing, print-on-demand fulfillment, digital product downloads, or dropshipping supply chains.

arrow_forward Typical Est. Startup: $0 - $5,000
02 Milestone 2

Select an E-commerce Platform

Weigh turnkey hosted convenience (Shopify, BigCommerce) against self-hosted open-source control (WooCommerce). Align tech abilities with time allocation.

arrow_forward Goal: Evaluate Free Trials First
03 Milestone 3

Set Up Secure Payments

Integrate redundant payment rails (Stripe, PayPal, UPI, or regional providers). Ensure mandatory SSL, PCI-DSS compliance checks, and clear checkout terms.

arrow_forward Focus: Checkout Security & Trust
04 Milestone 4

Organize Products & Inventory

Standardize your SKU schema, compose concise product descriptions with precise dimensions, configure tax categories, and upload high-fidelity product imagery.

arrow_forward Standard: High-Resolution Assets
05 Milestone 5

Configure Shipping & Logistics

Define transparent shipping zones: real-time carrier rates vs. flat fee models. Draft straightforward return, refund, and package damage resolution protocols.

arrow_forward Key: Clear Shipping Expectations
06 Milestone 6

Start Marketing & Automation

Install basic analytics and tracking before driving first traffic. Deploy automated cart abandonment workflows (Klaviyo) and engage your early customer base.

arrow_forward Milestone: First Store Transactions
